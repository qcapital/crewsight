Tips: pick reference turns the gun can reach without hitting its stops. The gun doesn't need to stay level: horizontal steps only measure side-to-side mouse movement, and vertical steps only measure up-down movement. Uneven swings or a turret stop only produce a warning, and if the vertical result is unreliable the horizontal scale is used for both axes.

    No gun limits recorded.
    
    
    Records exactly where the gun can point. Afterwards, your tracked aim stops at the same limits as the real gun, so pushing into a stop no longer makes your dot drift.
      How: land the helicopter (nothing else should move on screen) and share your game window on the Screen share tab.
      Then press Record, aim at the nose and press Mark (Ctrl+Alt+M). Slowly sweep the gun around the whole outer edge of its range (top edge, far side, bottom edge, other side), come back to the nose and press Mark again.
      Follow the edges closely and don't keep pushing into a stop. While your screen is shared, the app also detects when the picture stops moving and ignores that mouse movement.

    Crew dot on my screen (HUD)
    
  
    
    
    Draws your crew member's look/aim point directly over YOUR game, placed in your own view (Ctrl+Alt+H). Always click-through.
    
    
    
    
    
    
    Set FOV to the game's vehicle FOV (Settings → First Person FOV – Vehicles). A wrong FOV puts the dot in the right direction but too near or far from centre. Quick check: both look at the same object; the dot should sit on it.
    
  
    
  
    Pings
    
  
    
    
    Press your ping key (Hotkeys tab, default Ctrl+Shift+Space) in game: your crew member sees a marker where you are aiming, placed in their own view, plus a snapshot of your screen around your crosshair. Share your game window so snapshots can be taken.

    About & privacy
    

    CrewSight is an external companion. It never reads or writes game memory, never injects code or input, and never modifies game files.
    

    Sent to the relay: lobby code, your seat, viewpoint estimates (two angles + status, ~30/s), WebRTC connection setup, pings.
Sent to your crew member only (peer-to-peer, DTLS-SRTP encrypted): the video of the window you choose to share. No audio. Nothing is recorded.
    

    Mouse: only when "Observe my mouse" is on — relative counts are turned into an angle locally; raw counts are never sent or logged.
    

    The dot is an estimate. Read the state label next to it: MOUSE ESTIMATE, MANUAL, NOSE (ASSUMED), SIMULATED, STALE.
